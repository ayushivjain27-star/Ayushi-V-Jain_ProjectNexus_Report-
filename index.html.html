Assumptions & educational disclaimer

These figures are estimates for learning purposes only. They are not a guarantee, a forecast, or financial advice, and GoalBridge does not recommend any specific investment or product.

  • Inflation and expected return are assumptions you set, not guaranteed outcomes.
  • Current savings are assumed to grow at the same expected annual return.
  • You can choose whether each monthly contribution is made at the beginning or end of the month; the annual return is divided by 12 to get a monthly rate either way.
  • If your savings already cover the goal, the gap and contribution are shown as zero.
GoalBridge is an educational planning tool built for financial-literacy workshops. It does not collect or store any personal information — everything happens on this page.
